Compound interest pays interest on your interest, so the pot grows faster every year. Set a rate and a time, and compare it with simple interest, which only ever pays on the starting amount.

Compound: balance = P × (1 + r)t. Simple: balance = P × (1 + r × t). Interest is added once a year at a fixed rate, and the line joins the year-end balances smoothly. Leaves out tax, fees, inflation and rates that change.
