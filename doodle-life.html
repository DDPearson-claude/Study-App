Doodle Life draw it, it lives

Draw something. It comes alive.

Every doodle you finish gets a body and a mind of its own. The game guesses what you drew from its shape, and the creatures work out what to do with it.

  • Stick figure (a round head on top of a body) wanders, chats, sits, carries and kicks things.
  • Circle becomes a ball that rolls and bounces. Creatures love kicking it.
  • Chair, stool, box get sat on if they are the right height, carried if they are small, pushed if they are big.
  • A line is solid ground. Draw hills, ramps and floating platforms.
  • Sun, cloud, bird up in the sky stay there. Clouds rain. Birds fly.

Guessed wrong? Tap the label that pops up over a new doodle to change it. Use ✋ Grab to pick things up and throw them.
