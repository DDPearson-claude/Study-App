The Evenland

One continent, about 6,000 km from the Cold Coast to the Low Horn. Sixteen waters lie on one old water road, the Lamp Road, and each is joined to the last by a real river, canal, lock stair or stretch of coast.

Unrolling the chart…

IIIIIIIV
Uncharted land
the Lamp Road, by river, canal or lake
by sea
hauled up the Great Step
a kept lamp, one at each water
Old Murk’s lamp, and its twin
where the road runs on, unbought

A road chart, not a survey: the old kingdom is drawn about four times too large, and the Fold hides some 4,500 km of empty coast.
