Gloomwater Interface Directions

Three ways the game's menus, catch card and on-screen display could look. Each is drawn here in its real fonts and ornaments over actual game scenery, at real proportions. The phone frame shows how big the text will be on a phone.

Tell me A, B or C, or mix them (for example "A's menus with C's catch card", or "B but lighter"). I'll build your choice into the real game overnight and use it for every new screen.

Your colour reference

Taken from the screenshots you sent: a deep indigo-violet starry sky, lavender mist, a pink nebula glow, warm amber candle and torch light, gold autumn leaves and pale stone. The whole game moves to these colours (cave ceilings become starlit), and all three interface directions below now use them. The game scenery behind the mockups is only tinted to preview the change; the real lakes will be repainted properly, and the orange ring around the raft is being removed.

A · Drowned Filigree

No boxes at all. Menus float in darkness and a soft vignette; pale-ink reeds, fishing line and hook curls frame titles and the selected item, drawing themselves in. Big, widely spaced capitals; a warm, sturdy book serif for reading. The quietest and most elegant.

Fonts: Marcellus SC (titles) · Alegreya (text)

B · Lantern Iron

Heavy and tactile. Panels are held by hand-drawn wrought-iron scrollwork and rivets instead of thin lines; whatever you select glows like a candle and flickers. Carved, chunky lettering; sturdy text. Feels like old lantern ironwork and drowned harbour fittings. The boldest.

Fonts: Grenze Gotisch (titles) · Spectral (text)

C · Ink-Wash Field Journal

Hand-inked with brush and nib. Instead of boxes, content sits on feathered washes of dark ink; selections get a brush stroke, notes and measurements are handwritten, records are stamped in red. The menus read like an angler's private journal by lantern light. The most illustrated.

Fonts: IM Fell English SC (titles) · Crimson Pro (text) · Cedarville Cursive (handwritten notes)

What stays the same in every direction

  • The colours from your reference: indigo and violet, lavender text, pink for special moments, amber for your lantern and gold for trophies.
  • Much larger text than today, with buttons at least a fingertip tall on phones.
  • Original ornaments drawn from this game's world (hooks, line, floats, reeds, lantern iron, wax and ink), never copied shapes.
  • Fish shown here are placeholders; in the game each species keeps its own illustration.
  • The orange ring around the raft is being removed from the game.
