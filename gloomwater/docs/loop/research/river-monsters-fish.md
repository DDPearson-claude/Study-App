# River Monsters: every fish Jeremy Wade caught

*Compiled 13 November 2026 from episode transcripts, captions and episode guides. All nine seasons are researched; the independent second check has not been run.*

**What this covers.** 90 episodes and specials of *River Monsters* (Animal Planet, 2009 to 2017): 57 of the 57 regular episodes plus specials. So far it lists **about 172 distinct kinds of fish** he landed himself, plus 11 animals he caught that are not fish.

**How "caught" was decided.** A fish counts only when a source says Jeremy Wade landed it himself on screen, by rod, handline, net or by hand. Fish caught by guides or scientists, fish he hooked and lost, and animals he only saw are in the right-hand column. The evidence is mostly episode transcripts and captions, the official "Every catch by Jeremy Wade" season reels where they exist, Wikipedia's episode list and the fan wiki.

**Caveats.** A "(?)" marks an entry resting on one weak source. The specials are mostly re-edits of earlier episodes; no catches unique to them were found. Bait fish and by-catch are included where a source names them, so some small catches will be missing.
