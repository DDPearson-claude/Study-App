## 3. The Chart

### 3.1 The decision: the world map's bake is the Chart, its live layer is shared code, each water's sheet is the game's own

The world map generator (`tools/world-map/`) bakes the Waxwold once in Chromium (`terrain.mjs`, `ink.mjs`, `bake-client.js`:
relief, hydrology, coasts, hachures and plant marks) into one image (3016 × 4056 px at 2.6 px a Chart unit, 2.04 MB as
WebP), and its page (`page/app.js`, 80 KB) draws a **live layer** on a canvas over it: the border and cartouches, the Lamp
Road with its flow arrows, the lamps, the Landing's hearth, the landmark vignettes, the lettering, the Waystone arcs, and the
reveal of sheets by "maps owned".

**The Chart reuses all three, and adds one thing of its own:**

1. **The bake, as per-sheet crops.** The generator gains a game export (`node tools/world-map/build.mjs --game`): for each
   shipped sheet (WORLD §10's sheets, `canon.mjs SHEETS`), the bake of that sheet's box with its reveal mask as a feathered
   alpha, as WebP, at the game's resolution (reach I, the kingdom inset, at 5 px a unit so it is sharp at a phone's DPR 3;
   later reaches, seen from farther out, at 2.6), plus the paper texture. It writes one generated module, `src/chart-art.js`
   ("generated by `tools/world-map/build.mjs --game`; never edited by hand"): the crops as data URIs and the live layer's
   data for the shipped sheets only (their road legs, lamps, vignette pieces, labels pinned to those sheets, the Landing, the
   Waystone arcs). The game never bakes: no Chromium, no terrain code, no hydrology at runtime.
2. **The live layer, as shared code.** The drawing half of `page/app.js` (the lantern and hearth marks, the vignette builder
   `P()`, the road with its chevrons and out-and-back marks, the ink lettering, the frame and cartouches, the sheet ink-in)
   moves into **`src/chart-draw.js`**, a pure 2D-canvas module with no game imports. The game's Chart (`src/ui-chart.js`)
   calls it, and the generator's page inlines the same file at assemble time, so the published map and the in-game Chart are
   drawn by one code and can never drift. What stays in the page only: the journey animation, the road list, the slider, the
   design view of the whole world, pan and zoom (the game adds its own when a second sheet is owned).
3. **The water's sheet, game-native.** SPEC §5.4's sheet (the water's outline in ink, its places as vignettes or mist blots)
   is drawn by `ui-chart.js` from the game's own arena data (`MOSSMERE_V3`, `LOOP.places`, `world-layout`), in the
   generator's ink style, and pinned over the water's true place on the continent with a short ink stroke (WORLD §10: "each
   lake map is pinned at its true place … with a short ink stroke"). So the Chart's places sit exactly where the mist banks
   sit in the 3D world, and buying a place re-inks a vignette without any new image.

**Why not the alternatives.**

| alternative | why not |
|---|---|
| embed the published page whole (3.1 MB) | it would more than double the 2.69 MB game; it holds the whole continent's ink, which WORLD §10 forbids drawing below 16 of 16; it knows nothing of places, prices, the hold or Sail |
| a game-native redraw of the continent at runtime | the bake is about 110 KB of terrain, ink and hydrology code that takes seconds in desktop Chromium; on a phone it would be slow, and any drift from the published map would make two maps |
| a separate, hand-drawn Chart | it would not be "the map"; the user asked for the map |
