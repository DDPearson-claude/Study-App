# Doodle Life: improvement plan

**Diagnosis.** The engine is not the problem: JS frame work is 0.37 ms at 10 entities and 1.45 ms at 100, 60 Hz holds even with 308 bodies (p95 17.8 ms), the 180 s soak shows no heap growth, and no run produced a NaN or an off-screen body. The weaknesses are at the edges of the loop: recognition fails 11 of the 26 requested cases (lone circles r=12-18 are "seat" 6/6, the seeded demo ball is a ball in only 44/120 loads, one-stroke figures are "block" 6/6, a table is "ground" 6/6), and creatures spend 44-57% of their time in seek with about a third of seeks abandoned (17 give-ups in 120 s, 12 of them frozen above a lower target), while the dead-time share is 49.7% in the seed world. On top of that, several advertised features never fire (clouds never rain, the kick demo kicks in 0/10 loads, a hat drawn over a fresh creature retypes it to a ball 12/12) and first run is a hard block on landscape and small phones (help button at y=451 in a 390 px viewport).

## Quick wins

**Make clouds rain.** In `updateDecor` replace `if (e.rain < 0) return;` with `if (e.sub !== 'cloud') return;` and clamp `e.rain = 0` when the shower starts. Today `e.rain -= dt` leaves rain at -0.0067 and the sentinel exits every frame, so rain particles, `rainedOn()`, SAY.rain and the whole flee state are dead (0 particles in 15 s, 0 flees in 190 s). The help card promises "Clouds rain"; this turns on dormant content with 2-3 "brr"/"soggy" moments per shower. Optional layer 2: a swept drop test in `updateParticles` (40% of drops tunnel a 9 px roof) and flee away from the source cloud instead of toward screen centre. Impact 4 / effort tiny.

**Scale-aware cornerCount and a forced demo ball.** In `cornerCount` replace `resample(pts, 9)` with `resample(pts, clamp(max(bb.w, bb.h, 1) / 12, 3, 9))` and leave `round` as is. Measured: dead band r=10-20 becomes ball 8/8, pointer-drawn neat/wobbly/egg circles 20/20, 12%-overlap closures 14/20 (live 3/20), squares/pentagon/oval still thing. Do not add the isoperimetric term (it penalises overlapping ends, 0-1/5). Separately, in `seedWorld` pass explicit types (`makeEntity(ball, 'ball', o)` etc.) with `br = max(20, hs*0.16)`, show chips on first visit (`quiet: seen`, labelUntil + 6), and set `ai.firstTarget = ball` consumed by `decide()` so the first act is the kick. Forcing the type alone raises "kick within 10 s" from 0-1/12 to 6/10. Impact 4 / effort small.

**Unblock first run on phones.** CSS at L110-118: `.card { max-height: calc(100% - 32px); overflow-y: auto }`, `#help { align-items: safe center }`; add `helpEl.addEventListener('pointerdown', ev => { if (ev.target === helpEl) closeHelp(); })`; `#tray, .chip { touch-action: manipulation }` for the two-tap Clear. At 844x390 the card spans y=-120..510 and the only button sits off-screen with no Escape key on a phone. Leave tray redesign, accent colour and focus trap for later (the proposed 44 px tray measured 3 rows/165 px, worse than today). Impact 4 / effort small.

**Thought cloud over seeking creatures.** Set `ai.decidedAt = world.time` in `decide()` and add `drawThought(e)` after `drawBubble`: a 32x28 roundRect at the facing side of the head containing `goal.sprite` fitted to 20 px (goal = ai.target for seek/push/follow, carryGoal.t for carry), fading in after 0.15 s. Seek is 39-55% of walking time with a bubble in only 11-18% of seek samples, and seek and wander are visually identical. No text table, no tap gesture, no path line. Impact 4 / effort small.

**Persistence that survives closing the tab.** Delete the `dirty` flag; `save()` writes unconditionally on the 3 s tick and from `pagehide` and `visibilitychange` (hidden). Serialize `floorY`, `h`, `anchor`, `fy`, `ty` and restore `y = floorY - fy - h` (ground) or `ty*H` (sky), with a v1 fallback; scale platform strokes wider than W; `restoreWorld` returns true for any array and `start()` seeds only when `dl-world === null`; drop `!e.static` from `resize()`. Evidence: reload within 1.5 s loses the doodle, Clear then reload reseeds, a ground line floats 116-200 px after resize. Impact 3-4 / effort small.

**Stop the three stall modes, trimmed.** (1) In seek, when `feet(t) > feet(e) + 0.3h`, walk off the edge of `e.support` toward the target (fires only when the goal point would land on the support; persists until landing on another support) and count still seek ticks in `handleStuck` so freezes end in 2.4 s not 5-14 s. (2) In `resolvePair`, when both bodies are creatures set `ai.blocked` only on the smaller one: head-on passes go 0/5 to 5/5 in 1.7-2.0 s with one hop. (3) Move the `ai.blocked` clear from `physics()` L552 to `frame()` so substeps OR into it. Skip wall memory and wander clamps; the hopping at a wall invites a ramp. Impact 3 / effort small.

**Fix the five correctness bugs.** `updateBird`: `if (e.held) return;` and on release set `fly.baseY = clamp(e.y + e.h/2, H*0.06, floorY*0.5)`, `fly.vx = clamp(drag.vx, -120, 120) || fly.vx` (a grabbed bird currently ignores the hand). Extract `localX(e, wx)` from `surfaceY` and use it in `hitTest` (an erase on a left-facing creature's arm erased the chair). In the carry "give" branch re-check the L680 state predicate and call `standUp(o)` if `o.ai.seat` (a sitting receiver walks off owning the chair forever). In `removeEntity` handle `e.held === 'pointer'` and `closePicker()` if `pickerFor === e`; `undo()`/Clear call `release()` first (pageerror and alive=false zombies today). Impact 3 / effort small.

## Bigger improvements

**Stop the chip and picker hijacking strokes.** `.chip { pointer-events: none }`; in canvas pointerdown record `tapChip = chipAt(p)`; in `endPointer` open the picker only if the pointer moved < 6 px, else draw normally. Move the picker beside the doodle (`left = e.x + e.w + 12`, flipped near the right edge). Clamp chips in `updateChips` and flip them below when `e.y - 31 < 0` (chips at y=28 are fully off-screen). Widen the join margin above creatures to `0.3*e.h` so the hat joins instead of becoming floating "ground". Prototype: 0/12 retypes versus 12/12 live. Retype-any-time: a < 6 px tap in Grab opens the picker. Keep keyboard/ARIA and vocabulary as a separate low-priority change. Impact 4 / effort medium (core ~25 lines).

**Creatures that notice the player (tier 1).** In `decide()` multiply option weights by novelty (`novelUntil = quiet ? 0 : born + 12`, up to 3.5x) and scale pickup by 0.3 when `labelText === 'seat' && !t.sitter`; in `finalizePending` have idle/wander creatures within W*0.6 clear memory for the new entity, face it, say "curious" and re-decide. Measured chair time-to-sit: mean 22.4 s to 5.8 s, sit-first 17/20 (curiosity alone does nothing: 19.4 s). Route erase under a sitter through `standUp` with "huh", "sad" for a dead seek/follow target, a held wiggle in `drawEntity`, remove "hey!" from SAY.oof. Defer boredom requests and cooldown tables. Impact 4 / effort medium.

**Platforms that work.** In `makeEntity` set `e.snap = true` and use `tol = e.snap ? max(12, 0.15h) : 4` in the L497 landing test (feet touching a drawn line fall through today); `stepUp = e.ai ? 0.3h : max(0.12h, 8)` so a 5 px pen is never a wall for balls (balls at 80-600 px/s bounce off every ramp foot, 0 px climbed); balls only, after landing compute slope over +-e.r and when `|slope| > 0.15` add `vx += GRAVITY*0.6*slope*dt` and skip the |vx|<4 clamp (a ball on a 20-29 degree line sits still for 5 s; a wobbly flat line never reaches 0.15). Leave creatures sprinting up hills. Impact 4 / effort medium.

**Recognise more figure styles.** Add `splitLoopPrefix` (classification only: loop prefix with iq >= 0.7, tail >= 0.6*md, no Douglas-Peucker split), a topper excuse in the head loop (<= 2 non-arc strokes above the head within 0.45*lb.h) and the round-big-head exception, plus a mask-based `legY`. One-stroke head+body and whole-figure cases are "block" 6/6 today, hats 10-24 px above the head 6/6, chibi heads 6/6; the prototype fixed 14 families with 0 regressions on 89 shapes. Companion: in `addStroke`'s join branch pass `e.type === 'creature' ? 'creature' : null` as a soft prior. Impact 3-4 / effort medium.

**Surgical grouping fixes (contested, trimmed).** The ink-connectivity stroke graph was refuted (a 10 px connect threshold splits 15 px necks and 12-25 px arm gaps that work today), but the refutation itself keeps four edits: accept a stroke in `addStroke` if its first or last point lies within 12 px of the entity's ink (fixes head-then-pause, which currently yields seat + item at pauses >= 1 s); skip only `type === 'platform'`, not all static (sun faces keep working); never absorb a `round` loop into a creature or ball; in `classify()` count "above" ink only outside the head's x-range so hats stop disqualifying creatures; move `pending.last = world.time` so it fires only on a real join. Impact 3 / effort small.

**Thrown things hit, and a kick-back rally.** Generalise both bonk tests in `resolvePair` to `type === 'ball' || type === 'thing'` with `|rel| > 170` (creature speed caps at 150, so walking never triggers it) and tag `thrownBy`/`thrownAt` in `dropCarried` for a "sorry!" line. A box thrown at 500-800 px/s currently stops beside the target with no reaction. Then in `arrive('ball')` aim 60% of kicks at a nearby mate, set `expectBall` so the receiver poses "catch" and kicks back, "goal!" every fourth pass. Drop the bonds map (invisible, unpersisted, and it sours tone). Impact 3 / effort medium.

## Big bets

Nothing kept is large; every large proposal was refuted. The two items below are the most invasive kept changes because they touch shared contact code.

**Smooth motion: sit tweens, hop-off standUp, direction-aware landing.** Tween seek-to-sit over 0.25 s (`ai.tween`), make `standUp` a small hop (`vy = -0.35*jumpV`) instead of a 59 px blink, add a step limit in `resolvePair`'s rest branch and a decaying render `e.lift` for step-ups. Replace the `penY < 0.25h` rule with the same previous-bottom test `resolvePlatform` uses (`prevB <= sy + allow`), and never rest on an empty mask column (`sy === null`): a 229 px plank rode a ball across the screen, and a ball hangs 8 px off a box corner. Stock busy scenes show 24-26 one-frame moves > 40 px per 90 s; the prototype gives 2. Impact 3 / effort medium-to-large, needs a soak check.

**Rain shelter (layer 2 of the rain fix).** `roofOver(e)` picks the nearest column of an unheld platform/thing above the creature within `min(W*0.25, speed*1.5)`, seek with `act = 'shelter'`, "phew" on arrival; requires the swept drop test first. Impact 3 / effort medium.

## Considered and dropped

- **Static shape grammar** (tables, bird-anywhere, cloud/star templates): ~35 new thresholds replacing two taught rules; 12 regressions on held-out terrains; the chip already fixes misses. Keep only the speck guard (`maxd < 12` dropped with a poof) and one-stroke stairs as platform.
- **Classifier confidence and learned corrections**: conf values were per-category constants (every ball 0.30), the scorer sank suns and clouds, one mis-tap would relabel all same-signature doodles.
- **Adaptive LIFE_DELAY, dashed pending box, birth toasts**: taxes every doodle by 61%, hover rule can block birth forever; the rejoin rule above fixes the split instead. A faint pending ink alpha and a birth hop are fine.
- **Undo history with redo, names, share link**: unhosted file, no link to send; fix only the join undo (restore `e.prev` within 10 s) and add a picture button.
- **Toppling and lying hitboxes via stroke rotation**: both seats are "tall" by the rule and would become permanent lying blocks; sleeper fix as a pose swap only.
- **Richer thing affordances and hunger**: eating already exists via catalog tags; wire the inert `climb`/`ride`/`sit` tags and expose tags in the picker instead.
- **Procedural limb animation**: on six realistic figures it produced rigid pendulums or detached arms; a face/blink layer and a head slice are the robust subset.
- **Sound palette, pressure ink, night switch**: cornerCount is unaffected below 4400 px/s; OS-dark users would get a permanently sleepy world. Midpoint curves in `tracePath` and a born chime remain cheap options.
