THE COURSE (data, not instructions)
Topic: Linear systems you will actually use
Puzzle the course answers: Two batteries side by side feed one lamp, each through its own internal resistance. Both currents are unknown, and each one changes the other. How do you find the current each battery gives?

In one breath: A fact like x + y = 7 about two unknown numbers has a whole line of possible answers, and a second fact gives a second line, so the one pair that fits both is where the two lines cross. You can find that pair exactly, either by swapping one rule into the other or by adding the rules so a letter drops out, and you can tell when there is no single answer. Then you turn two facts from a story, or the rules for currents at a junction and voltages round a loop, into two equations and solve them.
