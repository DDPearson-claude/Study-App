A graph and its two shadows: the inputs it takes, down on the bottom axis, and the outputs it gives, across on the side. The 25% to 75% level, the −3 to 3 and −2 to 3 settings, the hump, and the 12 V supply with its 3 to 12 Ω resistance are examples.

Every shadow, beam and reading is worked out from the rule on its graph; the hump is a sketch.
