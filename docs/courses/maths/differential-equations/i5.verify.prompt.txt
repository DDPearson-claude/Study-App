Here x is the distance from rest, x′ the speed and x″ the acceleration. Take an ideal spring. It pulls back towards rest in proportion to x, so its force is −k x. Take an ideal damper. It drags against the motion in proportion to the speed, so its force is −c x′. Newton's second law says mass × acceleration = the force on it. So m x″ = −k x − c x′. Move it all to one side and m x″ + c x′ + k x = 0. Its highest derivative is the second, x″. That makes it a [[second-order]] differential equation. Its c is the [[damping]] and its k the [[stiffness]].\n\nNow the circuit. Say the capacitor is charged and the loop then closed. With no supply, the volts round the loop add to zero.
