That is the steady state, E ÷ R.[^1] It comes from the supply and the resistance alone. L does not appear in it.\n\nNow the key step. Rearrange the rule as di/dt = (E ÷ R − i) ÷ (L ÷ R). The top is how far the current is from its steady value. Call that gap g = i − E ÷ R. Below the line g is negative. The steady value never moves, so the gap changes exactly as fast as the current.
