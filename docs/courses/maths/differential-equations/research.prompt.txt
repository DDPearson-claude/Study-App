THE COURSE (data, not instructions)
Title: Differential equations, from cooling to resonance
In one breath: A differential equation is a rule for how fast something changes, written in terms of the thing itself, so its answer is a whole curve, not a number. One store of energy, such as a capacitor discharging or a coil switched onto a supply, settles smoothly at a pace set by one time constant, and a rule with no formula can be stepped along a little at a time. Two stores, such as a spring with a mass or a coil with a capacitor, share one equation whose roots decide whether it creeps back, returns quickest or rings, and pushing it in time with its ringing gives resonance.
Level: some
Ideas (id, title — one line, [kind]):
  i1  A rule for change — A differential equation gives a rate from the quantity itself, as cooling does, so its answer is a curve that follows the rule's slope everywhere. [concept]
  i2  Decay: separate and integrate — A rate in proportion to what is left, as in a discharging capacitor, separates and integrates to y = Ae^(−t/τ), with time constant τ. [skill] (needs i1)
  i3  Steady state plus a dying transient — Switch a coil onto a supply and its current is the steady value E ÷ R plus a gap that dies away as decay does. [quantity] (needs i1, i2)
  i4  One small step at a time: Euler's method — Euler's method walks short straight steps along the rule's slope, solving equations that have no formula, and smaller steps drift less from the true curve. [skill] (needs i1, i2, i3)
  i5  The spring and the circuit: one equation — A damped spring and series RLC circuit obey the same second-order equation, term for term: mass as inductance, damping as resistance, stiffness as 1 ÷ C. [quantity] (needs i1, i2)
  i6  Overdamped, critical and ringing — The roots of mr² + cr + k = 0 decide whether it creeps back, returns quickest without ringing, or rings inside a dying envelope. [quantity] (needs i2, i5)
  i7  Pushing it: resonance, and reading a model — Pushed back and forth, a lightly damped spring or circuit settles to a steady swing, largest when the push nears the frequency it rings at. [quantity] (needs i3, i5, i6)
