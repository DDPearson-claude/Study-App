Notes (claims, and the sources that support them):
- A function's derivative is zero where its graph has a horizontal tangent, at a turning point. [R1][R2]

- On a closed interval, a largest or smallest value occurs either at an endpoint or at an interior point, where it is a local extremum and so at a critical point. [R5]
- An HN engineering unit uses derivatives to locate turning points and to find the condition for maximum electrical power transfer. [R6][R7]
- The power rule: bring the power to the front, then subtract one from the power, so d/dx of xⁿ is nxⁿ⁻¹; the MCA syllabus covers ax^n for positive or negative n. [R8][R9][R10]  (from an idea this one builds on)
