1. **Explain, which side of the plane (one idea a sentence)**
   - Before: Off the plane, the step has a part along n, so the dot product is not 0. Its sign says which side. A dot product is positive when the step leans the way n points, and negative when it leans away.
   - After: Off the plane, the step has a part along n. So the dot product is not 0, and its sign says which side. It is positive when the step leans the way n points. It is negative when the step leans away.

2. **Explain, two planes meet in a line (no chained clause)**
   - Before: 2x + y + 2z = 9 and x − y = 0 have normals (2, 1, 2) and (1, −1, 0). Their cross product is (2, 2, −3). Check: it dots with (2, 1, 2) to 4 + 2 − 6 = 0, and with (1, −1, 0) to 2 − 2 + 0 = 0.
   - After: Take 2x + y + 2z = 9 and x − y = 0. Their normals are (2, 1, 2) and (1, −1, 0). The cross product of the normals is (2, 2, −3). Check it. It dots with (2, 1, 2) to 4 + 2 − 6 = 0. It dots with (1, −1, 0) to 2 − 2 + 0 = 0.

3. **Practice, the rule of thumb (lead with the condition, then the result)**
   - Before: If a line's direction dotted with the normal gives 0, the line never crosses the plane. It runs alongside or lies in it.
   - After: Suppose a line's direction dotted with the normal gives 0. Then the line never crosses the plane. It runs alongside it or lies in it.

5. **Analogy breaks (plain, active)**
