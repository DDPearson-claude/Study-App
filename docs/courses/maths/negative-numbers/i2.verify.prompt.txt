Later ideas in the course (every claim must stay true after Dan has learned each of them):
- i3 Adding — Adding a positive number moves you up the line, and adding a negative number moves you down.
- i4 Taking away — Taking away moves you down the line and on past zero when it must, so a change, last minus first, is negative when a reading falls.
- i5 Taking away a negative — Taking away a negative is the same as adding its opposite, so 3 − (−2) = 5.
- i6 Multiplying — Multiplying by a negative turns the line half a turn about zero, so one negative gives a negative answer and two give a positive.
- i7 Dividing — Dividing undoes multiplying, so 12 ÷ −3 asks what times −3 makes 12, and the signs follow the rule for multiplying.
- i8 Order with negatives — Work out brackets, then powers, then times and divide, then add and take away, so −3² is −9 while (−3)² is 9.
- i9 Formulas and your calculator — Put a negative reading into a formula inside brackets, and key it in with the (−) key, not the take-away key.
