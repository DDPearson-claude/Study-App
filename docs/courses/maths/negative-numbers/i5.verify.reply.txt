Taking away something negative takes you up the line, just as adding something positive does,[^3] so taking away a negative is adding its opposite, and 3 − (−2) = 3 + 2.\n\nThat is why last minus first works for a rise. From −20 °C up to +4 °C the change is 4 − (−20) = 4 + 20 = 24 degrees, the same 24 as the gap in lesson 2. It works for a fall too. One reefer cargo was loaded at around −5 °C,[^1] and the core of the stow took over 3 days to reach the −18 °C set point.[^2] Its change was −18 − (−5) = −18 + 5, about −13 degrees.\n\n**Taking away a negative is adding its opposite, so the marker faces down, steps backwards and goes up the line.**"
