With insulation resistance, what really matters is the trend over time, and a fall warns of problems coming.[^1]\n\nThe pile throws the order away. Lay the readings out in turn, and the order comes back. You have laid a line of best fit by eye before. Here the page fits the straight line that sits closest to all the readings at once. So a fitted slope has an uncertainty, like any result. When that range includes zero, there may be no drift at all.[^2] A real drift leans the line the same way, batch after batch.\n\nTwo more checks help. First, runs.
