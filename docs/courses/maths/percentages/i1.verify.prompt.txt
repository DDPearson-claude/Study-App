Later ideas in the course (every claim must stay true after Dan has learned each of them):
- i2 What percentage is it? — Divide the part by the whole and times by 100, so a 9 V drop on 230 V is about 3.9%.
- i3 Up or down by a percentage — Raise or cut in one step with a multiplier: up 20% is × 1.2, and down 15% is × 0.85.
- i4 Percentage change — A percentage change is the change divided by the original amount, times 100, so a fall comes out negative.
- i5 Back to the original — To find the amount before a rise or cut, divide by the multiplier, which is why up 20% then down 20% ends at 96%.
