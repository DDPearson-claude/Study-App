Later ideas in the course (every claim must stay true after Dan has learned each of them):
- i4 Triangles without a right angle — The cosine rule, Pythagoras with a correction −2ab cos C, and the sine rule solve triangles that have no right angle.
- i5 Radians: angle as arc over radius — A radian is the angle whose arc is one radius long, on any circle, so a whole turn, 360°, is 2π radians.
