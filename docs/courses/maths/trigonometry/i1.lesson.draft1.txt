Name the sides from the angle you are using, θ: the hypotenuse is across from the right angle, the opposite side is across from θ, and the adjacent side is the other one, next to θ. In the growing triangle θ is 30°. With a hypotenuse of 10 cm the opposite side is 5 cm, and with 20 cm it is 10 cm, so opposite ÷ hypotenuse is 0.5 both times. Turn θ to 40° and it becomes 0.643.\n\nThe three ratios have names. Opposite ÷ hypotenuse is the [[sine]] of θ, written sin θ. Adjacent ÷ hypotenuse is its [[cosine]], cos θ, and opposite ÷ adjacent is its [[tangent]], tan θ.[^1] SOH CAH TOA is a common way to remember which is which.
