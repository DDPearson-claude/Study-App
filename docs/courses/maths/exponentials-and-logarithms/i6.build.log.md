- Layout: the machine is one row of nodes holding the numbers handed on.
  - Forward steps sit above, and each undo sits below the step it reverses. This way every number
    is shown once and fits 340 px.
  - This differs from the brief's "each machine stacks its boxes in a column on a phone". The row
    keeps the mirror visible and needed no stacking.
- The time is shown twice on purpose: in the machine's t node and as the tag under the crossing.
  The brief's one thing is that the two agree.
- K.model returns share, lnShare and time from "level". All three show as labels on the drawing
  (the machine nodes), with no readouts.
- Every number on screen and in the text is computed from constants defined once (V0, C, R, the
  cooling and halving values) and rounded as the lesson writes it.
- Known wrinkle: in the font, "ln" on buttons and chips can look like "In".
