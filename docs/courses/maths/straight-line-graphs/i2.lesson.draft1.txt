    "text": "The step triangle between two points gives two more things besides the gradient. Its long side is the straight distance from one point to the other, and halfway along that side is their [[midpoint]]. To find the distance, plot the two points and draw the right triangle, much as you did for the gradient.[^1] The run and the rise meet at a right angle, so the long side is the hypotenuse, and Pythagoras gives its length, as in Geometry fundamentals. For A (1, 2) and B (7, 10), the run is 7 − 1 = 6 and the rise is 10 − 2 = 8. Square them, add, and take the square root: √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10. Adding the run and the rise, 6 + 8 = 14, goes round the two short sides instead, which is always longer.\n\nWritten with the coordinates, that is the [[distance formula]], d = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²), which comes from Pythagoras.[^2] Here, unlike the gradient, the order of the points makes no difference. Drag B across both axes to (−5, −6) and the run becomes −5 − 1 = −6 and the rise −6 − 2 = −8. A negative times a negative is positive, so (−6)² = 36 and (−8)² = 64, the same squares as before, and the distance is still 10. Starting from B instead only turns each difference's sign round, and its square is the same. Brackets still matter: from (−1, 4) to (5, −4), the run is 5 − (−1) = 6, not 4.\n\nThe midpoint sits halfway along the run and halfway up the rise. From A (1, 2), half the run is 3 and half the rise is 4, so the midpoint is (1 + 3, 2 + 4) = (4, 6). Halfway between two numbers is their average, so each coordinate of the midpoint is the average of the matching coordinates of the two points:[^3] (1 + 7) ÷ 2 = 4 and (2 + 10) ÷ 2 = 6. Halving the run and the rise alone, (3, 4), gives only how far the midpoint is from A, not where it is. With negatives the average works the same way: (−3, 5) and (5, −1) give ((−3 + 5) ÷ 2, (5 + (−1)) ÷ 2) = (1, 2). To work back to a missing end, take the same step again: from A (1, 2) to the midpoint (4, 6) is 3 along and 4 up, so B is at (7, 10).\n\n**The distance between two points is √(run² + rise²), Pythagoras on the step triangle,[^2] and the midpoint is the average of the two x's and the average of the two y's.[^3]**"
