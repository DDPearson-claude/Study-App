The area can be written as the two brackets multiplied or as the four tiles added, and these are two ways of writing the same area, so they are equal.[^2] Collect the two strips, 3x + 2x = 5x, and (x + 3)(x + 2) = x² + 5x + 6. At x = 4 the brackets make 7 × 6 = 42, and the tiles make 16 + 12 + 8 + 6 = 42.\n\nDrag the splits and the same thing always happens. For (x + a)(x + b) the strips are ax and bx, which add to (a + b)x, and the corner is ab, so (x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a + b)x + ab. Textbooks call an expression whose highest power of x is x² a [[quadratic]]. When both sides are x + 5 the rectangle is a square with two equal strips, so (x + 5)² = x² + 10x + 25. The common mistake is x² + 25, which leaves out both strips. With numbers, (1 + 4)² = 25, but 1² + 4² is only 17.\n\nA minus sign cannot be drawn as a length, so the rectangle becomes a grid. Each cell holds one term of the first bracket times one term of the second, with its sign. (x − 4)(x + 1) gives x², x, −4x and −4, so x² − 3x − 4.
