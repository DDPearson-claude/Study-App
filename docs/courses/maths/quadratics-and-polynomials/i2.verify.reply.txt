That leaves an a by b corner to fill with unit squares, so the rectangle closes only when the strips split into a pair that adds to 5 and the same pair multiplies to 6. This is lesson 1's (x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a + b)x + ab read from the right: to factorise x² + bx + c, the two numbers in the brackets must add to b and multiply to c.[^1] Of the whole-number pairs that make 6, 1 and 6 add to 7, and only 2 and 3 add to 5, so x² + 5x + 6 = (x + 2)(x + 3).
