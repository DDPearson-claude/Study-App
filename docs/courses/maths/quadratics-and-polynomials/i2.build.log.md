# i2 build log: Tiles into a rectangle

- Attempt 1 (i2.body1.html): 9 of 10 checks. The Bitesize check took the drag screen's opening
  rectangle, the (x + 3)(x + 3) slip, as that screen's answer. At Text size XL one long piece of maths
  that cannot break, `2x² + 10x + 12 = 2(x + 2)(x + 3)` on step 7, was 345 px wide in a 340 px frame.
  The pictures also showed the loose pile sitting above an empty band where the rectangle would go.
- Attempt 2 (i2.body2.html): the drag screen names its answer (x + 2)(x + 3). Maths in the text may
  now break at its = signs. The loose pile is centred in the space the rectangle will take.
  10 of 10 checks, no problems. Demonstrations settle by 3.4 to 4.2 s.
- Shape: 13 screens (S D S D S D S D S D S D D).
  - Rectangle screens (all-plus quadratics, steps 1 to 4 and 7 to 10): the tiles glide from a loose
    pile into the rectangle. A gap is drawn with a dashed red outline ("missing"). A tile the
    rectangle cannot use overhangs with a red outline ("left over"). A count table shows "needs ?,
    you have N", and the needs fill in when a rectangle is built.
  - Step 3 is the drag screen. It opens on the (x + 3)(x + 3) slip, which is missing 1 strip and
    3 squares, so it needs 6 strips and 9 squares. The knob at the end of each side sets that
    side's strips from 0 to 6. It closes only for 2 and 3, either way round.
  - Step 7 shows two copies of (x + 2)(x + 3), and step 9 has the group labels 2x(x + 3) and
    1(x + 3).
  - Grid screens (steps 5, 6, 11, 12 and 13) are filled in backwards. x² goes top left and the
    number on its own bottom right. The other cells and sides show ? until an answer, and a note
    says what the two ? cells must add to. A wrong pick fills in its own grid. Cells that do not
    match get a dashed red outline, and a note names the corner or x cells that differ.
  - Puzzles 6, 8, 12 and 13 also try a number (x = 4, 1, 1, 3). 6x(x + 1) on step 8 is checked by
    a number only, because its rectangle would need 6 x² tiles.
- Every count comes from the rectangle's slots. Every bracket and expansion is formatted from its
  numbers, and every value line is worked out from its expression. Nothing is typed in.
- For the reviewer:
  - The tiles draw x as 3.5 units, and the puzzle with Try x = 1 says "picture not to scale".
  - In steps 7 and 9 the rule rows appear only once the rectangle has closed. There is no "= ?"
    row before that.
  - The rectangle puts its longer bracket across the top, so on step 1 the top reads x + 3 and
    the side x + 2.
- Pictures read: all 11 from attempt 1. From attempt 2, the 7 the change touched: the opening shot,
  moved light and dark, and the contact sheets for steps 0, 2, 6 and 8. The grid end state and the
  contact sheets for steps 4 and 10 did not change.
- Token cost: about 0.33M tokens of context for this build (two attempts), against about 1.65M a
  lesson measured for the full process.
