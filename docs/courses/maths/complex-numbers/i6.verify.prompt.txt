- In polar form, to multiply you multiply the moduli and add the arguments. [R1]
- To divide in polar form, you divide the moduli and subtract the arguments. [R2]
- Multiplication and division are most easily done in polar form, while addition and subtraction are easier in Cartesian form. [R3]

- The argument is the angle between the positive real axis and the line from the origin to the point. [R7]  (from an idea this one builds on)
