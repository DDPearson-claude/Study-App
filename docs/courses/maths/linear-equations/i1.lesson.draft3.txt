Anything else stays side by side.\n\n**Worked example:** 6a + 5 − 2a + 3 − a. The a terms are 6a, −2a and −a, and 6 − 2 − 1 = 3, so 3a. The plain numbers are 5 and 3, which make 8. As a check, with a = 2 the start makes 12 + 5 − 4 + 3 − 2 = 14, and 3a + 8 makes 6 + 8 = 14.\n\n**Common mistakes**\n- Joining a letter term to a plain number, so 3x + 4 becomes 7x.
