Topic: Heat and fluids, from boiling water to pumps
His level: KNOWS A LITTLE: he has met the basics but may hold common misconceptions.
This lesson: i5 "Pressure in liquids, and floating" — Pressure in a liquid grows with depth alone, density × g × depth, and the extra push under an object is the upthrust that floats it. [mechanism]
Later ideas in the course (every claim must stay true after Dan has learned each of them):
- i6 Fluids in motion — Flow through a narrowing speeds up, and the energy for that speed comes out of the pressure, so a narrowed pipe, a venturi, measures flow.
- i7 Pipes, head and pump power — A pipe needs head, a height of liquid, for its lift plus friction that grows with flow squared, and head times flow sets pump power.
- i8 Where the pump settles, and its limits — A centrifugal pump settles where its curve of head against flow crosses the pipe's, so closing a valve moves it to less flow, more head.
