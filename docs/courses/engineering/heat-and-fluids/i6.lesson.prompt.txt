Topic: Heat and fluids, from boiling water to pumps
Puzzle the course answers: Partly close the discharge valve on a running centrifugal pump, and its motor usually draws less current, not more. Why does choking the flow make the motor's job easier?

In one breath: Heat is energy on the move from hotter to colder, and temperature is only one of the places it can go: it can melt or boil things instead, or push a piston, though an engine running in a repeating cycle can never turn all of it into work.
