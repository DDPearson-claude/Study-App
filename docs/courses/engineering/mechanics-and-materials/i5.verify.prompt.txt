Notes (claims, and the sources that support them):
- Work is force times the distance moved in the direction of the force, and it measures the energy transferred. [R1][R2]
- Power is work done per unit of time, so the same work done more slowly takes less power. [R3]
- One joule is the work of one newton moving through one metre. [R4]
- Rotational power is torque times angular velocity, and the speed must be in radians per second for the result in watts. [R5]
- A body keeps its motion at constant velocity unless a net external force acts, so constant velocity means zero net force, not a force pushing it along. [R6][R7]  (from an idea this one builds on)
- Acceleration is proportional to the net external force in the same direction and, as the full law states, inversely proportional to mass, so a net force changes velocity. [R8][R9]  (from an idea this one builds on)
- Work is force times the distance moved in the direction of the force, and one joule is the work done when one newton moves something one metre, so work is also newton-metres. [R1][R4]  (whole topic)
- A moment is measured in newton metres, force times perpendicular distance, the same unit product as work but a different quantity. [R10][R4]  (whole topic)
- Power converts to watts only with speed in radians per second, so a speed in rpm must be converted first. [R5]  (whole topic)
