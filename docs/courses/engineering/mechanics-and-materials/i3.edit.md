# i3 "Stress, strain and how materials fail": plain-English edit

50 sentences reworded in `i3.lesson.verified.json`, across the brief, whatAmILookingAt, ignores, explain,
analogy and practice. The same changes went into `i3.lesson.final.json` wherever it holds the same sentence.
Facts, numbers, units, formulas, [^n] citations, sources, ids and structure are unchanged; a script checked that
every number and citation in each field is the same before and after. The validator passes with `--final`.

## Five before-and-after examples

1. Talking about the course (explain)
   - Before: Remember each sling leg's tension in Forces in balance? This is what such a pull does to the metal.
   - After: A sling leg in tension carries just this kind of pull. Here is what that pull does to the metal.

2. Saying where it comes from (explain)
   - Before: Textbooks add that fatigue is the most common way shafts fail.
   - After: Fatigue is the most common way shafts fail.

3. Asides in the middle of a sentence (explain)
   - Before: A 10 kN pull on a 20 mm bar, with 314 mm² of section, gives 31.8 N/mm².
   - After: A 20 mm bar has 314 mm² of section. A 10 kN pull on it gives 31.8 N/mm².

4. An order to act, turned into sentences that read without controls (whatAmILookingAt)
   - Before: Let go anywhere. ... Let go at 10 mm and 360 N/mm², and it springs back 0.18 mm and keeps 9.82 mm. Drag again and the dot climbs that same line back to the curve.
   - After: You can let go anywhere. When you do, ... Let go at 10 mm and 360 N/mm² and the bar springs back 0.18 mm. It keeps 9.82 mm. Dragging again climbs the dot up that same line back to the curve.

5. Long chained sentences split (explain, bold key line)
   - Before: **Stiffness, yield and strength are three different things on one curve: the slope of its straight start, where it bends over, and the height of its peak.**
   - After: **Stiffness, yield and strength are three different things on one curve. Stiffness is the slope of its straight start. Yield is where it bends over. Strength is the height of its peak.**

## Notes
- The brief stays one sentence because the validator requires "The one thing you should see is ___ when you ___."
- `final.json` already differed from `verified.json` in four places (the word "usually" before the 0.2% offset,
  "many other metals", the springback's "times the 100 mm", "the stretch below yield"). Those differences were left as they were.
