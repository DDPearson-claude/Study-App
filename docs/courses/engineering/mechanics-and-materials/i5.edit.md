## Five before-and-after examples

1. Talking about the course (explain)
   - Before: Only the part of a force along the movement counts, as the dot product showed in Vectors.
   - After: Only the part of a force along the movement counts. The dot product of the force and the movement picks out that part.

2. Saying where it comes from (explain)
   - Before: Textbooks add that energy is never made or destroyed, only moved or changed in form.
   - After: Energy is never made or destroyed. It is only moved or changed in form.

3. A regrouping packed into one sentence of asides (explain)
   - Before: Regroup it and it is the load ÷ the effort, its mechanical advantage, over the effort's distance ÷ the load's distance, its velocity ratio.
   - After: Regroup it into two ratios. The load ÷ the effort is its mechanical advantage. The effort's distance ÷ the load's distance is its velocity ratio. The efficiency is the mechanical advantage over the velocity ratio.

4. An order to act, turned into sentences that read without controls (whatAmILookingAt)
   - Before: You drag the load to set its mass, from 100 to 1000 kg in 50 kg steps, opening at 500 kg. ... Press Lift and the load rises 10 m at that steady speed.
   - After: Dragging the load sets its mass, from 100 to 1000 kg in 50 kg steps. It opens at 500 kg. ... On Lift, the load rises 10 m at that steady speed.

5. A long chained puzzle sentence split, with the answer stated plainly (whatAmILookingAt)
   - Before: Screen 8 asks the efficiency of a winch that draws 800 W and puts 600 W into its load: 75%. Its wrong picks are 133%, the power in ÷ the power out, and 25%, the 200 W of losses ÷ the power in.
   - After: Screen 8 asks for the efficiency of a winch that draws 800 W and puts 600 W into its load. The answer is 75%. One wrong pick is 133%, the power in ÷ the power out. The other is 25%, the 200 W of losses ÷ the power in.
