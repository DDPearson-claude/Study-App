# i7 "Piping and system diagrams": plain-English edit

I rewrote 90 sentences in i7.lesson.verified.json. Most were long sentences chained with "and", "so", "while",
"which" or a colon. Each is now split into short sentences, one idea each, with the point first. The edits cover:
- explain, which now has six paragraphs instead of four. It is split at TV1, the cross-connection and the key rule.
- the analogy (text and breaks) and the practice.
- the interactive's whatAmILookingAt and ignores: the kit description, the rule, the screen words, the read
  sentences, the questions, the slips and the whys.

The read sentences are now full sentences ("Read: P1 is running. Water is lit from ..."), not lists of phrases, so
they make sense as stills in a read-through. "This lesson does not show" became "Nothing here shows", so the text
no longer talks about the lesson itself. A token check confirmed that every number, unit, formula, rule, [[term]]
and [^n] citation is unchanged. The only token change is a tag repeated where a pronoun was split off ("X1 is a
cross-connection", "From P3", "After S2", "you shut X1 too"). Sources, ids, numbers, controls and structure are
untouched. The brief already has the required "The one thing you should see is ___ when you ___" form, so I left
it as it was. The lesson has no predict, say or checks.

i7.lesson.final.json got the same rewrite (90 sentences). Its explain text still has its pre-fact-check wording in
three places: "the flow you trace ... through a running pump", "passes water one way only" and its shorter key
rule. In those places it kept its own facts and was only reworded in the same way.

The SHOW screens' words run from 27 to 42 words, well under 90. The validator passes on both files with `--final`.

## Five before-and-after examples

1. A relative clause and a list of steps, both inside one sentence (whatAmILookingAt, the return line)
   - Before: "The water then comes back past the line from the header tank HT1, which keeps the loop full and gives the
     water room to expand, through V5 and the strainer S1, and splits to the two pumps' suctions."
   - After: "The water then comes back past the line from the header tank HT1. The tank keeps the loop full and gives
     the water room to expand. The water runs on through V5 and the strainer S1, and splits to the two pumps'
     suctions."

2. A read sentence made of phrases, turned into plain sentences (screen 8)
   - Before: "Read: Isolate on, P2 picked, its suction pipe red to V3 and its discharge pipe red through NR2 to V4."
   - After: "Read: Isolate is on and P2 is picked. Its suction pipe is red to V3. Its discharge pipe is red through NR2
     to V4."

3. The point first, the exception after (explain, the drawn state)
   - Before: "Unless the sheet says otherwise, it shows each valve in its normal position.[^1]"
   - After: "The sheet shows each valve in its normal position, unless it says otherwise.[^1]"

4. Passive voice made active (screen 14 question)
   - Before: "S2 must be opened to clean it. What is the fewest valves you must shut?"
   - After: "You must open S2 to clean it. What is the smallest number of valves you must shut?"

5. One fact per sentence (screen 11 why)
   - Before: "It cuts the loop off from its header tank, and the drawing gives no sign of it. As the water warms it
     expands, and with nowhere to go the loop's pressure climbs."
   - After: "It cuts the loop off from its header tank. The drawing gives no sign of it. As the water warms, it
     expands. With nowhere to go, the loop's pressure climbs."
