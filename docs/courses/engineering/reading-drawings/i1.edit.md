# i1 "How any diagram talks": plain-English edit

40 of the original sentences were rewritten. Most became two shorter ones. The changes cover explain, analogy
(text and breaks), practice, and the interactive's whatAmILookingAt and ignores. Every number, unit, rule,
tag, [[term]] and [^n] citation is unchanged. The same rewrite is in i1.lesson.final.json, which keeps its
own wording where it differed (the lamp and tag sentences in explain, the crossing line in practice, the hump
line in ignores). The validator passes on both files with `--final`.

## Five before-and-after examples

1. Source narration removed (explain, drawn state)
   - Before: "Textbooks add that a relay or contactor contact is drawn as it sits with its coil off."
   - After: "A relay or contactor contact is drawn as it sits with its coil off."

2. Source narration removed and a trailing clause split off (explain, piping)
   - Before: "Textbooks add that a piping diagram usually shows each valve in its normal position, the one for the system's usual flow."
   - After: "A piping diagram usually shows each valve in its normal position. That is the position for the system's usual flow."

3. Colon list made into short sentences, point first (explain)
   - Before: "Take a small pump set: a supply, an isolator, a starter, a motor and the pump it drives, with a pipe to a tank."
   - After: "Take a small pump set. It has a supply, an isolator, a starter, a motor and the pump it drives, with a pipe to a tank."

4. Mid-sentence aside taken out of a read sentence (whatAmILookingAt, screen 4)
   - Before: "Read: the motor's cable lights with the starter and the motor, and the lamp's line, crossing it with no dot, stays dark."
   - After: "Read: the motor's cable lights with the starter and the motor. The lamp's line crosses it with no dot, so it stays dark."

5. Colon clause chain broken up (interactive.ignores)
   - Before: "The light marks one line and the parts it touches, not current: current also flows on through closed contacts and switches."
   - After: "The light marks one line and the parts it touches. It does not mark current, which also flows on through closed contacts and switches."

Kept as it was: the brief keeps the validator's required shape, "The one thing you should see is ___ when you ___."
