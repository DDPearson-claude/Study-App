## Five before-and-after examples

1. Tautology removed and the sentence split (explain, the drawn state)
   - Before: "Unless the sheet says otherwise, each contact is drawn in its drawn state, with its coil de-energised and no button pressed."
   - After: "Unless the sheet says otherwise, each contact is shown in its drawn state. That means its coil is de-energised and no button is pressed."

2. Show screen split into short sentences, point first (whatAmILookingAt, screen 6)
   - Before: "In bold, each contact here shows what it is doing now, over a faint copy of how it is drawn. With the tank full, -K1 is in, so its contacts already differ from the drawing."
   - After: "Here each contact shows in bold what it is doing now. A faint copy under it shows how it is drawn. The tank is full, so -K1 is in. Its contacts already differ from the drawing."

3. Read sentence with three clauses made into three sentences (whatAmILookingAt, screen 8)
   - Before: "Read: Start 1 has pulled -Q1 in and its latching contact has held it, then -F1 has opened and -Q1 has dropped out, with its three contacts back as drawn."
   - After: "Read: Start 1 has pulled -Q1 in, and its latching contact has held it there. Then -F1 has opened and -Q1 has dropped out. Its three contacts are back as drawn."

4. "since" clause made its own sentence (explain, current paths)
   - Before: "Check the line that feeds it too, since Stop -S0 in path 2 also feeds path 4."
   - After: "Check the line that feeds it too. Stop -S0 in path 2 also feeds path 4."

5. Cause put before effect (practice, worked example)
   - Before: "Coil -Q1 is in, because -M1 runs, so that contact is open."
   - After: "-M1 runs, so coil -Q1 is in, and that contact is open."
