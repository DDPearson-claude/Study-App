# i5 build log: The sliding valve

- Attempt 1 (passed): 11 of 11 checks, the sweep was clean, and the verdict was ok.
  Films: screen 1 settles by 4.0 s (the boxes light in turn, then P, T, A and B, then the 4/3 label),
  screen 5 by 2.4 s (the row is pushed to the left box, the rod rises, then the springs bring the

  No popping. The words fade in as each demonstration settles.
- Design choices beyond the brief:
  - The whole symbol slides, solenoids and springs included, against fixed ports. This is how the symbol
    is usually animated. The row snaps from box to box when dragged, as "What the model leaves out" says.
  - Pressing -Y1 switches -Y2 off, and the other way round. A drag overrides the solenoids while held.
    On release the solenoid in use, or the springs, take the row back.
  - Under the pump, a line in the picture shows "feeding the cylinder", "unloading to the tank" or
    "The pump's oil goes over the relief valve". The relief valve's arrow slides into line when it opens.
  - The "stopped here" mark shows only on screens 9 and 10, while a box holds the rod. With Leaks on, the
    rod creeps at 0.05 of its travel each second until it is fully in. That is about 10 s from half out.
    The after-answer creep on screen 10 is left out of the 3 s settle check on purpose.
  - Screen 6's two lever valves are stacked one above the other so the slide fits at 360 px.
    The brief's "side by side" is kept as a comparison, not as a layout.
  - Screen 8 shows the lone P to T box. After a right answer it gives way to the main circuit,
    and the centre picker then moves from Closed to P to T.
  - Screens 3 and 9 have no automatic demonstration because their words ask Dan to act.
    Their stills set -Y1 on with the rod coming out, and Leaks on with the rod below its mark.
    Their `read` sentences describe those stills.
  - The six cards are buttons in an HTML grid, two to a row at phone width. Each shows "Tap to open"
    until it is opened. Screen 11's demonstration opens the 3/2 card.
- Token cost: one build attempt, with no repair prompt.
