# i8 "Engineering drawings": plain-English edit

I rewrote 47 sentences in i8.lesson.verified.json. Most were long sentences joined with "and", "so", a colon or a
trailing "-ing" clause. Each is now split into short sentences, one idea each, with the point first. The edits cover:
- explain: the views rule, the line types, the section paragraph, the sizes paragraph and the key rule.
- the analogy (text and breaks) and the practice.
- the interactive's brief, whatAmILookingAt and ignores: the bracket, the rule, the switches, the phone layout, and
  the screen words, read sentences and whys.

The read sentences on screens 3, 5, 9 and 11 are now full sentences, so they make sense as stills in a read-through.
"A short lesson in fourteen small screens" became "Fourteen small screens follow", so the text no longer talks about
the lesson itself. A token check confirmed that every number, unit, symbol (Ø, ±, ÷, A–A), [[term]] and [^n] citation
is unchanged. Sources, ids, numbers, controls and structure are untouched. The lesson has no predict, say or checks.

i8.lesson.final.json got the same rewrite where it holds the same text (45 sentences). It still has its pre-fact-check
wording in a few places: "A solid line", no "usually" in the views and practice lines, the shorter analogy, and the
Unfold sentence about the back wall. Those places kept their own facts and were reworded the same way.

The SHOW screens' words run from 37 to 45 words, well under 90. The validator passes on both files with `--final`.

## Five before-and-after examples

1. Three clauses in one sentence (whatAmILookingAt, the rule)
   - Before: "The front view shows width and height, the view from above shows width and depth, and the view from the
     left end shows depth and height."
   - After: "The front view shows width and height. The view from above shows width and depth. The view from the left
     end shows depth and height."

2. A colon carrying a second idea (whatAmILookingAt, the Unfold button)
   - Before: "An Unfold button opens the box out flat round the wall the front view lies on: the back wall in first
     angle, the front wall in third."
   - After: "An Unfold button opens the box out flat round the wall the front view lies on. That is the back wall in
     first angle and the front wall in third."

3. A list folded into the middle of a sentence (explain, the lines; screen 7 likewise)
   - Before: "A thin chain line, long dash, short dash, long dash, marks the centre of a hole or a slot."
   - After: "A thin chain line marks the centre of a hole or a slot. Its pattern is long dash, short dash, long dash."

4. A read sentence made of a phrase chain (screen 9)
   - Before: "The section A–A shows the base and the upright hatched, with a gap in the upright where the hole is."
   - After: "In section A–A, the base and the upright are hatched. The upright has a gap where the hole is."

5. A key rule run together with a comma and a colon (explain, last line)
   - Before: "**Read the projection symbol first, then follow each feature across the views: each view shows two sizes,
     and together they fix the shape.**"
   - After: "**Read the projection symbol first. Then follow each feature across the views. Each view shows two sizes,
     and together they fix the shape.**"
