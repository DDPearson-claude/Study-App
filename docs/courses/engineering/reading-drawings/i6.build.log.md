# i6 build log: The press

- Attempt 1 (failed, 9 of 10 checks): the check on ram motion failed. Rounding errors left the ram a hair short of the
  work (0.7999999999999993). It still stalled there because the rule's test allows 1e-9, but it never reached the work
  exactly. The self-test also gave advice that "B" and "4/3 valve" read as one label.
- Attempt 2 (passed): 10 of 10 checks, sweep clean, verdict ok. Fixes: the ram now lands exactly on the work or at the top.
  The "4/3 valve" label moved below the valve's right end, well clear of B and T.
  Films: screen 2 settles by 4.0 s (eight parts light in turn: the job, pump and motor, tank and filter, relief valve,
  gauge, valve, the flow control and check valve pair, cylinder). Screen 6 settles by 2.9 s (the ram comes down past the
  flow control, stalls on the work, then the relief valve and gauge light). No popping. Words fade in as each
  demonstration settles.
- Design choices beyond the brief:
  - Screen 1's right answer squeezes the small cylinder from 300 to 200 cm³, with a heavier load and 9 bar absolute.
    The press does not replace it on that screen. It first appears on screen 2, so the picture Dan has just solved stays
    in view while he reads Why?. Screens 2 and 3 show the press with no flow lit. Lit lines would answer screen 3's
    question early.
  - Spare oil over the relief valve is a thinner bold line, and it is in the key. While the ram comes down, the gauge
    sits on its relief mark, because spare oil is already going over the relief valve. At the stall, all the oil goes
    that way, so the relief line turns fully bold. The gauge has one mark and no numbers. Its needle is hidden on
    puzzle screens until they are answered.
  - Screen 5 is an order puzzle. Five named part buttons sit beside the picture, and each tap rings that part on the
    drawing. The want is Flow control, 4/3 valve, Tank. Hints follow the brief: on the check valve, on the pump, and
    otherwise. Start over clears the taps.
  - Stop switches both solenoids off and stops the pump. The stored line is red with the label "still under pressure":
    A if the ram is on the work, else the whole B side. Tapping a solenoid while stopped does nothing. The say line then
    asks for Stop again first. The buttons are never disabled, because the kit's disabled style would fail contrast.
  - The air card is its own view on screens 10 and 11. The squeeze test sits below the 5/2 circuit, not beside it, so it
    fits at 360 px. Screen 10's read says "below it". The slider is a K.control with id "bar", labelled "Load", running
    from 6 to 12 bar absolute. The readout "The air fills" is computed as 1800 ÷ pressure. A dashed mark shows the air
    rod's height at 6 bar.
  - Every DO step has a `still` that settles its answered state, for the guide. Screen 5's still taps the parts in order.
- Token cost: two build attempts and one small repair (a rounding snap and a label move). The cost was not measured
  inside this agent. Take it from the workflow's own usage report.
