## Five before-and-after examples

1. **Explain, the law of reflection**
   - Before: "That line is the [[normal]], and both angles are measured from it. The angle the ray arrives at, the angle of incidence, equals the angle it leaves at, the angle of reflection.[^1]"
   - After: "That line is the [[normal]]. Both angles are measured from it. The angle the ray arrives at is the angle of incidence. The angle it leaves at is the angle of reflection. The two are equal.[^1]"

2. **Explain, turning the mirror**
   - Before: "A ray 25° from the surface is 90 − 25 = 65° from the normal, so it leaves at 65° on the other side. ... The angle between the arriving and leaving rays is the two angles together, so it changes by 20°."
   - After: "A ray 25° from the surface is 90 − 25 = 65° from the normal. So it leaves at 65° on the other side. ... The angle between the arriving and leaving rays is the two angles added. So it changes by 20°."

3. **Screen 6, where the image sits (words and read)**
   - Before: "Your eye cannot tell that light bounced. ... That spot is the image, as far behind as the object is in front." Read: "Its dashed line back still ends at the same image, 40 cm behind the mirror, with the lamp 40 cm in front."
   - After: "The image sits as far behind the mirror as the object is in front. Your eye cannot tell that light bounced. ... That spot is the image." Read: "Its dashed line back still ends at the same image. The image is 40 cm behind the mirror, and the lamp is 40 cm in front."

4. **Screen 13, the periscope (why)**
