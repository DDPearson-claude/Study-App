## Five before-and-after examples

1. **Explain, counting ways to move**
   - Before: "Three of those ways, two slides and a turn, move the whole frame without changing its shape, and the supports stop those. Each of the other ways changes the shape, and each bar stops one. Each way left to move is a [[degree of freedom]], and a frame with one left can fold."
   - After: "Three of those ways move the whole frame without changing its shape. They are two slides and a turn, and the supports stop them. Each of the other ways changes the shape. Each bar stops one of them. Each way left to move is a [[degree of freedom]]. A frame with one left can fold."

2. **Explain, the forces at a foot**
   - Before: "At a foot, the same bar's across part is 10 × cos 30° = 8.66 kN, pushing the foot outwards. The roller cannot push sideways, so the bottom bar must pull the feet together, with 8.66 kN. ... Pointing away from the pin, the bar is in [[tension]]. Pointing into the pin, it is in [[compression]].[^4]"
   - After: "At a foot, the same bar's across part is 10 × cos 30° = 8.66 kN. It pushes the foot outwards. The roller cannot push sideways. So the bottom bar must pull the feet together with 8.66 kN. ... If it points away from the pin, the bar is in [[tension]]. If it points into the pin, the bar is in [[compression]].[^4]"

3. **Screen 3, counting ways to move (read)**
   - Before: "The triangle has 3 joints and 3 bars, and 2 × 3 − 3 = 3, so it holds. The square has 4 joints and 4 bars, one short of 2 × 4 − 3 = 5, so it folds."
   - After: "The triangle has 3 joints and 3 bars. It needs 2 × 3 − 3 = 3, so it holds. The square has 4 joints and 4 bars. It needs 2 × 4 − 3 = 5, so it is one short and folds."
