That amount of field through the coil is its [[flux]]. A changing flux through a coil makes a push round it, and the push is set by how fast the flux changes, not by how much there is.[^2] A push made this way is called an [[emf]], measured in volts like any voltage. Face-on, the flux is at its most, and for that instant it is neither growing nor shrinking, like a ball at the top of its throw, so the emf is zero.
