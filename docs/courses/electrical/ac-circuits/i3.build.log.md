- Every number is computed from the card's R and peak:
  - i = v / R and p = v x i.
  - Average = (Vp / sqrt2) x (Vp / sqrt2 / R).
  - Peaks and ticks come from num(Vp), Vp / R and Vp^2 / R.
  - Cards: 10 ohm / 100 V peak; mains 52.9 ohm (230^2 / 1000); clamp 23 ohm (230 / 10); ship 44 ohm (440 / 10) at 60 Hz; "tops", a power-only card sized so its humps top out at 3000 W.
