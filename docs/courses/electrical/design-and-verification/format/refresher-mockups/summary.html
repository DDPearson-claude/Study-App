Design current, Ib

What it is for

The current the circuit carries in normal service (slides p.11). The device, the cable and every check after them are sized from it.

The rule

Single-phase: Ib = P ÷ (U × eff × pf)

Three-phase: Ib = P ÷ (√3 × UL × eff × pf)

P is the output. U is the voltage at the board when you are given one (slides p.12, 13, 18).
