Steps 7–8

Voltage drop

VD = mV/A/m × Ib × L ÷ 1000

Add every drop from the origin. The total must be within 3% (lighting) or 5% (other circuits) of the supply voltage you were given.

Why

The load needs its volts to work as designed, and the limit is for the whole path, so the submain's drop counts as much as the final circuit's.
