## 1. What it is for (page, screen 2)
- Before: The cable's resistance uses up some of the supply's voltage on the way, and the load gets what is left: the voltage drop (your slides p.38, 39). Table 4Ab limits it for an installation fed from the public supply, as your slides give it (p.38): lighting up to 3%, 6.9 V single-phase and 12 V three-phase; other circuits up to 5%, 11.5 V and 20 V, from the origin to the most distant point of the circuit (Total Skills). BS 7671 takes the percentages of the nominal voltage (NAPIT); your course takes them of the supply voltage when one is given (p.40), the stricter way when it is below nominal.
- After: The cable's resistance uses up some of the supply's voltage on the way. The load gets what is left: the voltage drop. On a public supply, Table 4Ab limits it. Lighting may drop up to 3%: 6.9 V single-phase and 12 V three-phase. Other circuits may drop up to 5%: 11.5 V and 20 V. The limit runs from the origin to the most distant point of the circuit. BS 7671 takes the percentages of the nominal voltage. In your assessments, take them of the supply voltage when one is given. That is the stricter test when it is below nominal.

## 7. Explanation, the Ct refinement (lesson JSON, explain)
- Before: Current heats a conductor and a hotter conductor has more resistance,[^24] so a cable carrying well under its rating runs cooler and drops less,[^25] and a factor, [[Ct]], corrects the drop for it. Your slides' heater cable, chased up to 6.0 mm², still carries just 13.04 A of the 41 A it is rated for,[^21][^22] so it drops less than its 8.09 V, and with Ct the smaller 4.0 mm² may pass.[^57][^33]
- After: Current heats a conductor, and a hotter conductor has more resistance.[^24] So a cable carrying well under its rating runs cooler and drops less.[^25] A factor, [[Ct]], corrects the drop for this. Take a 3 kW heater cable chased up to 6.0 mm². It still carries just 13.04 A,[^21] and it is rated for 41 A.[^22] So it drops less than its 8.09 V.[^57] With Ct, the smaller 4.0 mm² may pass.[^33]
