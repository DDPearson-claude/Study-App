## 1. Explain: the closing key sentence
Before: "**Distance is time × velocity ÷ 2: a reflection up means the impedance rose, one down means it fell, and the velocity is set on a known length.**[^5]"
After: "**Distance is time × velocity ÷ 2. A reflection up means the impedance rose. One down means it fell. Set the velocity on a known length.**[^5]"

## 2. Screen 3: the parallel-fault working in one long sentence
Before: "Two resistances in parallel give less than either, so the impedance falls and the reflection points down. Put that parallel pair into the rule and it becomes ρ = −Z0 ÷ (2 Rf + Z0), where Rf is the fault's resistance. The higher Rf, the less it pulls the impedance down, and the smaller the dip."
After: "Two resistances in parallel give less than either. So the impedance falls, and the reflection points down. Call the fault's resistance Rf. Put the parallel pair into the rule, and it becomes ρ = −Z0 ÷ (2 Rf + Z0). The higher Rf, the less it pulls the impedance down. So the dip is smaller."
