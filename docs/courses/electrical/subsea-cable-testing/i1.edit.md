Only the wording changed. Every number, unit, rule, figure id and [^n] citation is the same.
The same edits were made in i1.lesson.final.json wherever it holds the same text. Where its older
sentence differs, it got the matching edit in its own words. One common-mistake line was already
plain in the final file, so it was left alone there. 49 sentences were rewritten across the explain,
the analogy, the practice, the screen walk-through, the "what it leaves out" note, the Section B
points and model answer, and the summary card. `validate.mjs lesson ... --final` passes on both

## 1. Screen 3: a semicolon list of controls turned into short sentences
Before: "The controls: a Length slider from 0.40 to 2.40 km in steps of 0.20 km, opening at 1.00 km; a Switch off at the peak button; an Earth switch, opening open."
After: "There are three controls. A Length slider runs from 0.40 to 2.40 km in steps of 0.20 km, and opens at 1.00 km. A Switch off at the peak button. An Earth switch, which starts open."

## 2. Screen 5 read sentence: the point first, then the working
Before: "Read: earth both ends and both are held at earth, while a current circulates instead. With only the OSS end earthed, the B01 end sits at the induced voltage: 96 V over 2.40 km, at the practice 40 V per km."
After: "Read: with only the OSS end earthed, the B01 end sits at the induced voltage. That is 96 V over 2.40 km, at the practice 40 V per km. Earth both ends and both are held at earth. A current circulates through the earths instead."

## 3. Practice: a stacked practice-value aside moved into its own sentence
Before: "Switched off at the peak of U0, 38 kV here, a practice value, E = 0.528 × 10⁻⁶ × 38 000² = 762.43 J."
After: "Take U0 as 38 kV, a practice value. Switched off at the peak, E = 0.528 × 10⁻⁶ × 38 000² = 762.43 J."

## 4. Screen 3: one sentence carrying three events split into three
Before: "Opening the earth straight after: the faint marks drift back out to the plates, the bar creeps back up over a few seconds, and in red, “It has recharged from the insulation. Earth it again and leave the earth on.”"
After: "Open the earth straight after, and the faint marks drift back out to the plates. The bar creeps back up over a few seconds. A red line says: “It has recharged from the insulation. Earth it again and leave the earth on.”"
