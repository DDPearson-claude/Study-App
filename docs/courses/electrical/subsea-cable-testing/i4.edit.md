## 5. Analogy: a colon list and a long "without" clause
Before: "On an installation you pick the IR test voltage to suit the circuit: higher for a 400 V circuit than for SELV. The aim is a test that stresses the insulation enough to show a fault, without asking more of it than it was built for. The sheath test matches its voltage to the sheath in the same way, by its thickness."
After: "On an installation you pick the IR test voltage to suit the circuit. A 400 V circuit gets a higher test voltage than SELV. The test should stress the insulation enough to show a fault. It should not ask more of it than it was built for. The sheath test does the same thing. It matches its voltage to the sheath's thickness."
