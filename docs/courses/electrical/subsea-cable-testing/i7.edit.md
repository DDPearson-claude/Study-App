## 2. Screen 4: the echo's working, with x defined at the end of a clause
Before: "The echo comes back after the round trip, 2x ÷ v, where x is the spot's distance. Because it is late, it slips in and out of step with the main signal as the frequency rises. In step it adds, out of step it takes away, so it puts a small ripple on the spectrum."
After: "Call the spot's distance x. The echo comes back after a round trip of 2x ÷ v. The echo is late. So it slips in and out of step with the main signal as the frequency rises. In step, it adds. Out of step, it takes away. So it puts a small ripple on the spectrum."
