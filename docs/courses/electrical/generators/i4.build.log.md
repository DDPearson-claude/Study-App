## Choices worth knowing
- The governor runs exactly as the brief's rule says (12% per hertz, 12% a second per hertz, 0.1 s wait,
  0.1 s lag, -10% to 110%, time slowed three times). Simulated in 2 ms steps. A 25% step from 60 Hz reads 58.9 Hz
  0.1 s later (58.93), bottoms at 57.1 Hz 1.1 s after the tap on screen, and settles in about 2.1 s of model time.
- Slowed three times, a step takes about 6 s on screen to settle, a trip about 11 to 14 s. So the film's 5 s sheets end
  before the tail. The isochronous recovery has one small second sag (to 59.4 Hz), the rule's own tuning.
- The trace's clock runs only while the set is changing, and stops once it has settled (within 0.1 Hz and 1% fuel;
  the last bit then snaps to the line, under a pixel). So "the last 6 s" means the last 6 s of change.
- The flywheel's mark is drawn as a strobe at 1800 rev/min shows it. It stands still at 1800 rev/min, slips back in a
  dip and creeps forward when fast. That makes "the flywheel slows" visible; a mark turning in proportion would show
  a 5% slowdown as nothing. A label on the drawing and the caption say so. Off 60 Hz the mark keeps creeping, so the
  droop screens (8 to 12) never settle on film. That is why steps 8, 10 and 11 are in lookAt.
- Load is five setting buttons. A tap jumps straight to the chosen setting, so 0 to 100% in one tap is possible. It dips
  to about 47 Hz, and the trace and the dot are clipped at their axes then. The brief's 25% steps stay inside the axes.
- Screen 6 has no auto demonstration: its words describe the steady 25% state, and its trace opens showing screen 4's
  dip. Its read-through still runs three steps 1.8 s apart, so all three dips fit in the 6 s window.
- The droop graph labels 56, 60 and 64 Hz (ticks every 2 Hz), so it shows no unlisted number such as 62.
