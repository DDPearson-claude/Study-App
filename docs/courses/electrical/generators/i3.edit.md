## Five before-and-after examples

1. Explain: a sentence that narrated where a fact comes from now just states it.
   - Before: "Textbooks add that a generator's synchronous reactance is far larger than a transformer's reactance."
   - After: "A generator's synchronous reactance is far larger than a transformer's reactance."

2. Picture notes: a semicolon chain becomes four short sentences, one part of the circuit each.
   - Before: "At the top, one phase of the set drawn as a small circuit, left to right: a circle marked E, the voltage the set makes inside; a coil marked Xs, the synchronous reactance; and the terminals marked V, with the load beyond."
   - After: "At the top is one phase of the set, drawn as a small circuit. On the left, a circle marked E is the voltage the set makes inside. Next comes a coil marked Xs, the synchronous reactance. On the right are the terminals, marked V, with the load beyond."

3. Explain: the long sentence that named Xs is split, so the new term gets its own sentence.
   - Before: "Both grow with the load current, so together they act like one reactance in series with each phase, the [[synchronous reactance]], Xs.[^1]"
   - After: "Both grow with the load current. Together they act like one reactance in series with each phase. This is the [[synchronous reactance]], Xs.[^1]"

4. Screen 7 words: a squeezed clause with a missing verb and a tacked-on description become plain sentences.
   - Before: "E's part along V falls to 0.1, and E to 1.20. Step on to Zero pf leading, the current a bank of capacitors draws."
   - After: "E's part along V falls to 0.1. E falls to 1.20. Step on to Zero pf leading. That is the current a bank of capacitors draws."

5. Screen 10 why: two "so" chains become one step per sentence, so the working reads in order.
   - Before: "Its part across V is 1.2, so its part along V is √(3.24 − 1.44) = √1.80 = 1.34. The drop takes 0.9 of that, so V = 1.34 − 0.9 = 0.44."
   - After: "Its part across V is 1.2. So its part along V is √(3.24 − 1.44) = √1.80 = 1.34. The drop takes 0.9 of that. So V = 1.34 − 0.9 = 0.44."
