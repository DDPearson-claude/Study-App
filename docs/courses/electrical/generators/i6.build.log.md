## Choices worth knowing
- The rule is the brief's own, solved exactly. Each set runs in one of four states: on its droop line, at full
  fuel (504 kW, so the frequency can fall further), with its fuel cut to nothing at its own setting (driven by 0 to
  50.4 kW), or above its setting and drawing the full 50.4 kW. So the shares stay continuous as a setting is dragged
  past the other set's. Isochronous follows the brief: the higher setting takes the load plus 50.4 kW, up to 504 kW.
  Past that, the frequency falls to the lower setting.
- Screen 3 sets δ to 32.3° itself, and works the power the way the lesson does. It takes 2.25 × sin 32.3° ÷ 1.5,
  rounds it to 0.80 per unit, then multiplies by 630 kVA to give 504 kW. Worked from the exact sine it would read
  505 kW, which would contradict the lesson's "504 kW". A check states this working.
- The kW and kVAr graphs share one horizontal scale for the same load: 0.3 px per kW and 0.4 px per kVAr, so
  600 kW and 450 kVAr are the same width. Every 4% screen uses a 4 Hz window. Screen 8, at 1% droop, uses the same
  4 Hz height, so its lines look flatter, as the text says. Screen 9, isochronous, zooms to 0.5 Hz, so the 60.1 and
  60.0 Hz lines sit clearly apart. A window slides, and widens only if it has to, when a free control carries the
  crossing or a setting outside it.
- Axis numbers: only "60 Hz" or "440 V" is labelled. The other ticks are unlabelled, so the page shows no
  number the lesson does not list.
- A ring marked "before" keeps the screen's opening crossing on screens 5, 7 and 14 once answered, and on 6, 8,
  11 and 12. That way the kW moved and the small rise in frequency are both visible.
- In the kVAr view, a red dashed arrow along the board shows the cross current. It flows from the set with more
  kVAr into the other, at a speed that follows the current, and it fades to nothing when compensation is on. So
  screens 12 and 13 never settle on film. Compensation trims both AVRs over about 1.5 s. Their lines ease to 447.5 V,
  and the crossing moves to 225 kVAr each at 440.0 V.
- Free controls follow the brief: A's setting on 6; Droop and A's setting on 8; Compensation on 12. Two additions:
  on screen 9, once answered, the Governors choice and the Load become free (so the Load stepper the brief
  describes is reachable). On screen 14, once answered, A's and B's settings become free. The view switch is never
  shown as a control. Screens 10 to 13 flip it themselves.
- Every SVG label has a page-coloured halo, so a label stays readable where a line crosses it.
- The body is 68 KB, over the prompt's 40 KB guide. i4's final body was 60 KB and i5's was 66 KB.
