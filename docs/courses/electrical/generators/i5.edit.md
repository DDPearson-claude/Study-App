explain, analogy and its breaks, and practice. Every fact, number, unit, rule, formula and [^n]
citation is unchanged. Sources, ids, numbers and structure are untouched. The brief keeps its

## Five before-and-after examples

1. Screen 6 words: a surge sentence with its value tacked on, and a torque figure hung off a comma, become short steps.
   - Before: "That gap drives a surge limited only by the reactances of the set and the board, 5.7 per unit here. It also yanks the rotor into step, 4.0 per unit of torque through the shaft and coupling."
   - After: "Only the reactances of the set and the board limit the surge that gap drives. Here it is 5.7 per unit. The surge also yanks the rotor into step. That puts 4.0 per unit of torque through the shaft and coupling."

2. Screen 8 words: the new term was buried mid-sentence between two asides. It now gets its own sentence.
   - Before: "Instead, send the close early, at the [[advance angle]], 3.6° before 12.[^3]"
   - After: "Instead, send the close 3.6° before 12. That lead is the [[advance angle]].[^3]"

3. Explain: a sentence with an aside in the middle ("the same size") and a trailing tag splits into three.
   - Before: "If the set's wave, the same size, runs θ degrees ahead, the pole sees 2 sin(θ ÷ 2) × the phase voltage, the gap you met across a paralleling switch."
   - After: "Now say the set's wave is the same size but runs θ degrees ahead. Then the pole sees 2 sin(θ ÷ 2) × the phase voltage. It is the same gap you met across a paralleling switch."

4. Practice: a step that narrated where the advice comes from now just gives it, keeping it as usual practice rather than a rule.
   - Before: "Textbooks add that you leave it turning slowly towards FAST, so the set picks up a little load as it closes."
   - After: "It is usual to leave it turning slowly towards FAST. Then the set picks up a little load as it closes."

5. Screen 14 question: three facts and a rounding instruction in two sentences become one idea a sentence.
   - Before: "The set runs 0.3 Hz fast and the breaker takes 80 ms. How many degrees before 12 does it send the close, to one decimal place?"
   - After: "The set runs 0.3 Hz fast. The breaker takes 80 ms. How many degrees before 12 does the relay send the close? Give it to one decimal place."
