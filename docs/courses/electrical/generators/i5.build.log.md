## Choices worth knowing
- The angle moves in real time at 360 × the slip degrees a second, with no slowing. At 0.2 Hz it moves 72° a second.
- A close is computed exactly. The press lands at the script's exact time, and the shut angle is the press angle
  plus 360 × slip × 0.05. The relay finds the exact moment the pointer crosses the advance angle short of 12, so
  its breaker shuts at 0° (to 1e-12).
- After a shut, the pointer swings back to 12 over 0.5 s, eased. The bars grow over the same 0.5 s, and the shut dot
  fades in. That is the surge yanking the rotor into step, so nothing pops in. The readouts jump to their shut
  values (Angle 0, Slip 0.00, Across pole A 0, Incoming 440), and the lamps go dark at once.
- DO screens keep their picture still until the answer is right, so the question stays true while he reads it.
  Then the answer plays:
  - Screen 5 times one turn at 0.25 Hz. A teal arc sweeps round and the key reads "one turn in 4 s".
  - Screen 7 plays the pointer from 180° round to 12 at the true 0.05 Hz, so it takes 10 s.
  - Screen 9 presses at 12 and shuts 9° FAST.
  - Screen 11 shuts at 0° with B and C crossed: 6.9 per unit and "Wrong sequence".
  - Screen 13 ramps the trims to 440 V and 60.10 Hz. The two trims then show, free to play with.
- Screen 7 also hides the Across pole A tile until checked. That tile would read 508 V and give the answer away.
  The brief did not ask for this.
