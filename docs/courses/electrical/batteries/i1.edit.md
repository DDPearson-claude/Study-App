1. **Explain, the current through the liquid**

   - After: "Inside the liquid, [[ions]] carry the current. Ions are the acid's charged pieces. One plate gives out electrons and the other takes them in. Left alone, charge would pile up beside each plate. The ions drift through the acid and keep it balanced. That closes the loop."
