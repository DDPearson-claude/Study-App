So the liquid gets lighter and more like water as the charge falls.[^4] Its [[specific gravity]] measures that. It is the liquid's density divided by water's, the relative density you met with floating. Water reads 1.000. A hydrometer draws some acid into a glass barrel with a float inside. The float sinks until the acid it pushes aside weighs as much as it does. In denser acid it pushes aside less, so it rides higher. It is why a ship rides higher in seawater. The scale reads in points, and one point is 0.001. Say this stationary cell's maker marks full charge at 1.225. The level matters too. Water evaporates but the acid stays, so a low cell reads high.
