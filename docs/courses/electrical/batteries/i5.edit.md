`validate.mjs lesson ... --final` passes on both files.

## Five before-and-after examples

1. **Explain, why gas comes near full**
   - Before: "That is why gas comes faster as the battery nears full, and freely if charging carries on past it.[^8]"
   - After: "That is why gas comes faster as the battery nears full. Keep charging past full, and it comes freely.[^8]"

2. **Explain, the float-current trap**
   - Before: "Sized from a float current of 0.5 A, the same opening would be 36 cm², forty times too small."
   - After: "Size the opening from a float current of 0.5 A instead, and you get 36 cm². That is forty times too small."

3. **Interactive, screen 8**
   - Before: "At 0.1 m/s, 1440 cm², which is 0.144 m², passes 0.0144 m³ a second, or 14.4 litres."
   - After: "1440 cm² is 0.144 m². At 0.1 m/s it passes 0.0144 m³ a second, or 14.4 litres."

4. **Interactive, ignores**
   - Before: "The climb after the fan stops, and the current at which a sealed cell's valve lifts, are sketches with time sped up."
   - After: "The climb after the fan stops is a sketch, with time sped up. So is the current at which a sealed cell's valve lifts."

5. **Analogy, where it breaks**
   - Before: "A fault on a circuit is cleared by its protective device within seconds, often a fraction of one. ... Its current can run for hours and the gas keeps coming, so the vents must cope for as long as it lasts."
   - After: "On a circuit, the protective device clears a fault within seconds, often in a fraction of one. ... Its current can run for hours, and the gas keeps coming. So the vents must cope for as long as the fault lasts."
