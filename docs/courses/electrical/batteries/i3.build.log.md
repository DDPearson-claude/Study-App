- The three outputs are drawn on the picture, not as K.readout tiles, because the screen text prints their values. They are the lost-volts label, the voltmeter, and the counter's ampere-hours once a run has finished. So no row of readout tiles needs to wrap at 360 px. The answer buttons are in a `minmax(6rem, 1fr)` grid, which becomes one column when an answer is long.
- Every number comes from the rule. E = 6 × 2.1. r runs on a straight line from 7 mΩ at 27 °C to 14 mΩ at −18 °C. The capacity share runs from 1 at 27 °C to 0.5 at −18 °C. n = log(20 ÷ 2) ÷ log(28 ÷ 5) = 1.34. The run time is t = 20 × (5 ÷ I)^n × share, and the ampere-hours are I × t. The load's R = (E − Ir) ÷ I. Halving R uses I = E ÷ (R + r).
