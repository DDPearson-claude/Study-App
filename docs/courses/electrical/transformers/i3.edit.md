## 2. Explain, Lenz's law: one direction per sentence
Before: Close a circuit on the secondary, and its current makes its own field. That field pushes against the change. So it points against a rising flux, and with a falling one. If it helped the change, the change would feed itself and make energy from nothing.

After: Close a circuit on the secondary, and a current flows. That current makes its own field, and the field pushes against the change. Against a rising flux, it points the other way.
