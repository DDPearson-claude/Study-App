Current in the primary drives flux round the core, through the secondary's turns. Change that current, and the flux changes. Each turn of the secondary then gets an emf, as the turning coil did. But nothing turns here. The flux itself grows and shrinks. One coil's changing current making an emf in another is [[mutual induction]]. The emf in each turn equals the flux's rate of change. The turns are in series, so their emfs add: e = N × ΔΦ ÷ Δt. This is [[Faraday's law]]. Hold the current steady, and the flux is steady.
