## 1. Explain: name the buck converter first
Before: "The circuit is a switching transistor followed by a diode, an inductor and a capacitor.[^2] This is a
[[buck converter]]."
After: "A [[buck converter]] is a switching transistor followed by a diode, an inductor and a capacitor.[^2]"

## 2. Screen 4's read sentence: one idea a sentence
Before: "with D at 50% and the output at 24 V, the area above the line, 24 V for half the cycle, equals the area
below, 24 V for the other half."
After: "with D at 50%, the output sits at 24 V. The area above the line is 24 V for half the cycle. It equals the area
below, 24 V for the other half."

## 3. The picture: the current plot without a "because" chain
Before: "It ramps up while the voltage is above the line and down while it is below, because a coil's current
changes at its voltage ÷ L amps a second, from v = L di/dt."
After: "It ramps up while the voltage is above the line. It ramps down while the voltage is below. The coil's current
changes at its voltage ÷ L amps a second, from v = L di/dt."

## 4. Explain: the boost balance stated, then solved
Before: "The same balance, Vin × D = (Vout − Vin) × (1 − D), gives Vout = Vin ÷ (1 − D)."
After: "The same balance holds: Vin × D = (Vout − Vin) × (1 − D). That gives Vout = Vin ÷ (1 − D)."

## 5. Practice: lead with the rule, then its limit
Before: "While the inductor's current never falls to zero, the frequency sets the ripple, not the output voltage."

zero."
