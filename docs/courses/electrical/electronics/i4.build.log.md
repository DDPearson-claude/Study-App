  parts, keyed under the circuit.
- A bar running 0 to 3000 W would show switching's 20 W + 20 W split as about one pixel. So the bar's scale is
  the thermometer's. Half on (3000 W) runs it off the top, and the hump plot shows the whole 40 to 3000 W climb.
- The strip has two settings:
  - Switching: the switch's voltage (0 to 1000 V scale, a 600 V reference line) and the current over a 0.3 ms
    window. The edges are drawn slowed down as straight sweeps, each with a red flash band that brightens as the
    cursor passes. The cursor sweeps once in 3.6 s.
  - Coil: 0 to 2 ms, opening at 0.4 ms. With no diode, a red spike runs off the top with a "spike" label. With
    the diode, the voltage steps to 601 V and the coil current dies away round the diode. A bracket marks
    "L ÷ R = 0.33 ms". With a resistor, the voltage simply steps to 600 V. On the coil screens the gauges are
    left out and the circuit is centred.

  - The coil current round the diode uses its 0.7 V drop.
