Only the wording changed. Every number, unit, rule, formula, [[term]] and [^n] citation is the same. A script
checked each field before and after, counting every number, symbol, term and footnote. Sources, ids and structure
are untouched.

65 sentences were rewritten in i5.lesson.verified.json, through 57 edits. That covers the picture, the screen words,
the read sentences, the puzzle hints and whys, interactive.ignores, the explain text, the analogy and where it breaks,
and the practice. The same edits were made in i5.lesson.final.json, which holds the same sentences. final's own

## 1. Screen 4: name the idea first
Before: "The angle in each half where the gate fires is the firing angle, α. Drag it. … Fire later and the shaded
slice shrinks."
After: "The firing angle, α, is the angle in each half where the gate fires. Drag it. … Fire later, and the shaded
slice shrinks."
Its read sentence now has one idea a sentence: "at 0°, the whole positive hump is shaded. The average reads 104 V.
At 90°, only its second half is shaded. The average reads 52 V."

## 2. Screen 6: the controlled rectifier said as a fact, not an order
Before: "Swap the four diodes of a bridge rectifier for thyristors, and it becomes a controlled rectifier. … The
average is (Vmax ÷ π)(1 + cos α), where Vmax is the 325 V peak."
After: "A bridge rectifier with thyristors in place of its four diodes is a controlled rectifier. … The average is
(Vmax ÷ π)(1 + cos α). Vmax is the 325 V peak."

## 3. Screen 10: one job a sentence for the choke
Before: "Here the bridge feeds it through a choke, a large coil, which carries the current through each zero. So each

After: "Here the bridge feeds it through a choke, a large coil. The choke carries the current through each zero. So
each pair conducts on past 180° until the other pair fires. The part below the line subtracts. The average becomes
207 × cos α."

## 4. Explain: the charger's average and what lies past 90°
Before: "The average then becomes (2Vmax ÷ π) × cos α, about 207 V × cos α on 230 V. Past 90° it would turn negative,
with power flowing from the DC side back to the supply.[^9]"
After: "The average then becomes (2Vmax ÷ π) × cos α. On 230 V that is about 207 V × cos α. Past 90° it would turn
negative. Power would then flow from the DC side back to the supply.[^9]"
The paragraph on areas now ends at "So cutting it costs little." The controlled bridge starts its own paragraph.

## 5. Practice: the safety line and the worked example
Before: "Lead-acid batteries give off hydrogen on charge, which is explosive in air from 4.1 to 72 percent.[^7]" and
"The peak is 325 V, so fired at 0° it gives 2 × 325 ÷ π = 207 V. So cos α = 120 ÷ 207 = 0.58, and α is about 55°."
After: "Lead-acid batteries give off hydrogen on charge. Hydrogen is explosive in air from 4.1 to 72 percent.[^7]" and
"The peak is 325 V. Fired at 0°, it gives 2 × 325 ÷ π = 207 V. So cos α = 120 ÷ 207 = 0.58. That makes α about 55°."
