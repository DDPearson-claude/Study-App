CHAPTER II · EQUAL PIECES MAKE THE BOTTOM NUMBER HONEST

Put it into practice

RULE OF THUMB

whole ÷ piece.

Fits 5 times? It is 1/5.

WORKED EXAMPLE

a 50 m drum cut into 20, 10, 10 and 10 m runs. 50 ÷ 10 = 5, so each 10 m run is 1/5. No quarters.

COMMON MISTAKES

Reading the bottom as pieces you can see.

Judging equal by eye, not measuring.
