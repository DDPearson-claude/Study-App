II CHAPTER II OF VIII CONCEPT

Equal pieces make the bottom number honest

KEY IDEA

Count how many of the piece fit the whole, not the pieces you see.

THINK OF IT LIKE

Three phases don't make each a third of the load; equal loads do. With 30 kW in total, a 20 kW phase is no third.

WHERE IT BREAKS

A board's total load changes through the day; a cut metre of cable stays put.
